How often should a home be pressure washed?

Annual exterior cleaning is a common maintenance schedule, especially in Florida's humid climate. Timing varies by property and buildup.

Jax Pro's current site describes its cleaning solutions as biodegradable and its soft-washing process as designed for safe exterior cleaning when properly applied.
